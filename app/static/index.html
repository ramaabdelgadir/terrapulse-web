A walk inside TerraPulse.

A real walk through the platform: choose a measurement, pick a place, read the verdict, compare places, check the evidence, ask Kemet, and save what you find. It is a screen recording of the live site working on real NASA data.

02 · Instruments on board

What can you explore?

Five measurements: four from NASA satellites, one from a thermometer on the ground that keeps the satellites honest. Each colour pair is the one the map uses for “less” and “more”.

03 · Method

How does TerraPulse work?

  1. 01

    Choose what to explore

    Pick a measurement and a place: a preset region, a single map cell, or a box you draw yourself.

  2. 02

    Investigate the observations

    TerraPulse builds the monthly time series from cleaned NASA data and tests it with the seasonal Mann-Kendall test, so the yearly cycle can’t fake a trend.

  3. 03

    Understand the evidence

    You get a plain-language verdict, the rate of change with its 95% range, and an honest label when the evidence isn’t strong enough.
